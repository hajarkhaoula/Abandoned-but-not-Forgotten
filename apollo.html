01 — THE BEGINNING

What Was the Lunar Module?

The Apollo Lunar Module (LM) was a spacecraft designed by NASA to transport astronauts from lunar orbit to the Moon's surface and return them to the Command and Service Module. It was used during the Apollo missions between 1969 and 1972.

02 — HISTORIC MISSIONS

Three Famous Lunar Modules

APOLLO 11 · 1969

Eagle

Eagle made the first crewed Moon landing on July 20, 1969. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface.

APOLLO 12 · 1969

Intrepid

Intrepid landed in the Ocean of Storms on November 19, 1969, carrying Charles Conrad and Alan Bean.

APOLLO 15 · 1971

Falcon

Falcon landed on July 30, 1971, carrying David Scott and James Irwin. Apollo 15 introduced the Lunar Roving Vehicle.

03 — ENGINEERING

Two Stages, One Mission

Descent Stage

The lower section helped the spacecraft land on the Moon. It contained the landing engine, fuel tanks and landing legs. It remained on the lunar surface.


Ascent Stage

The upper section contained the astronauts' cabin and ascent engine. It carried the astronauts back into lunar orbit to reunite with the Command Module.

04 — THE LEGACY

What Remains?

The descent stages of the Apollo Lunar Modules remain at their lunar landing sites. They are silent reminders of one of humanity's greatest achievements.
